# Hold this binary off the stores

**Do not submit `clicks-user` 1.1.6+16 from this working tree to the App Store or Play Store.** The SOS cash journey works on production — login, pin confirm, dispatch, track, cash QAR 150, rate — but the binary that would actually be archived cannot be Play-signed, would ship without Firebase push, cannot recall a bad first build, and still contains five customer-visible defects that a same-day QA branch fixed only in an unpushed worktree. Play upload fails first on missing `android/key.properties` and `clicks-release.jks`; a forced IPA would archive without `GoogleService-Info.plist`, without an app-level `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`, and with a hosted privacy policy that claims to cover Android apps only. Chat, a customer wallet, and dark mode are absent and are **not** launch blockers. What blocks a listing is packaging, legal copy, ops config, and defects that strand a driver after the van has left. Merge `fix/customer-qa-2026-11-13`, restore Firebase and signing secrets on the build machine, rewrite the privacy and deletion URLs, confirm the support number, and hide or honestly enable card pay — then a watched soft launch is coherent. A public store push from this checkout is not.

## Do not ship this working tree

The 13 November 2026 lifecycle QA rated a **Chrome web build** against production **SHIP WITH CAVEATS** ([qa-customer-lifecycle-2026-11-13.md](clicks-api/scripts/qa-customer-lifecycle-2026-11-13.md)). That verdict does not transfer to a store binary. The same note says the test was “not the store binary,” skipped push, GPS prompts, and the PDF viewer, and recorded five bugs whose claimed fixes live on unpushed `fix/customer-qa-2026-11-13` — **not pushed, not merged, not deployed**. This checkout still awaits `FCMNotificationService.deleteToken()` with no timeout on logout ([settings_screen.dart](clicks-user/lib/features/settings/ui/view/settings_screen.dart)), still always draws Pay now while production `flags.skipcash=false` ([job_in_progress_screen.dart](clicks-user/lib/features/home/job_in_progress_screen.dart)), still has no `SCHEDULED_OVERDUE_GRACE_MS` in the booking guard ([customerBookingGuard.js](clicks-api/clicks-shared/utils/customerBookingGuard.js)), and still does not refresh Activity on tab tap ([home_cubit.dart](clicks-user/lib/features/main/ui/cubit/home_cubit.dart)). There is no first-run permission primer. Welcome is brand plus Get Started. Location is requested the first time SOS is created. ## Play cannot sign this tree; Apple can archive a dangerous IPA

Android `applicationId` is `com.roya.clicks_user`; iOS bundle is `com.roya.clicksUser` (team `TVSQU4RKRZ`). The mismatch is expected; Firebase and App Store Connect records must use the iOS string exactly ([build.gradle.kts](clicks-user/android/app/build.gradle.kts); [project.pbxproj](clicks-user/ios/Runner.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj)). Main manifest permissions are `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `CAMERA` — no background location, `allowBackup=false`, `usesCleartextTraffic=false` ([AndroidManifest.xml](clicks-user/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml)). ATS is default HTTPS; release `AppConfig` refuses non-HTTPS API URLs ([app_config.dart](clicks-user/lib/core/config/app_config.dart)). There are **no** App Links, Universal Links, custom URL schemes, `apple-app-site-association`, or `assetlinks.json`. Job deep-linking is FCM tap routing only. SKAdNetwork IDs are absent and **not required** (no ads) ([SKAdNetworkItems](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/BundleResources/Information-Property-List/SKAdNetworkItems)). No StoreKit / IAP; SkipCash is an external WebView. Enabling iOS membership as a digital subscription via SkipCash later collides with Apple paid-digital-content rules — a future risk while flags are off, not a current reject.

Would **fail to build or upload**: missing Android signing files; missing `play-service-account.json` if they use the Triplet `play` block (track is `alpha`); iOS plist referenced but absent; `targetSdk` 35 after 31 August 2026; possible 16 KB block on native `.so`; missing/invalid privacy manifests on a commonly used SDK. Fill App Privacy nutrition labels in Connect for precise location, contact info, and identifiers ([Apple App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)). ## Conclusion

The customer app is a complete cash-SOS product trapped in an incomplete store package. Web QA proved the spine; the AAB script, missing Google services files, Android-only privacy HTML, technician deletion page, null support phone, and unmerged cold-start freeze prove the listing is not that spine. The decision that changes the verdict is not “build chat.” It is: merge the 13 November QA branch, restore Firebase and signing on the build machine, tell Apple and Play the truth about 30-day deletion and iOS, hide Pay now until card is actually on, and put a human and a real number at the end of the eight-minute SOS ladder. Until those are done, `1.1.6+16` from this tree stays off both stores.
