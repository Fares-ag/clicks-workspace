## Bottom nav, journey screens, and unfinished states

### Takeaway
Live SOS/job takeover is real (full-screen routes leave the tab shell), but several customer-facing details still look unfinished: missing names, `"N/A"`, 0-star ratings from a stale list, technician free-text reused as Job ID, dispatcher issue text shown as Service Details, dead Call buttons on one in-progress entry path, and a Pay Now card button that still appears when SkipCash is off.

## Empty, error, offline, GPS denied, coverage, high demand

### Takeaway
High-demand, poor-GPS, SOS-off, splash-offline, notifications-empty, and My Cars-empty are designed screens or banners. Most other failures — outside coverage, network, SkipCash off, cancel, payment — are floating SnackBars. GPS-denied has **no** dedicated “open Settings” screen: createSOS catches the permission error, falls back to a Doha pin, and asks the customer to drag it.
