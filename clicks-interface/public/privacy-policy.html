Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 — Effective August 3, 2026

Introduction

Sanad RSA ("Clicks", "we", "us") operates the Clicks Technician mobile application and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we access, collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use the Clicks Technician app and associated dispatch platform.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, phone number, email, profile photo, vehicle details, and login credentials.
  • Location data: precise GPS coordinates from your device.
  • Job and activity data: job status, timestamps, photos, notes, earnings, and service history.
  • Device and app data: device model, operating system, app version, push notification token, and diagnostic logs needed to operate the service.

Location data — including background location

The Clicks Technician app requests access to your device location, including background location (location when the app is closed or not in use), when you set your status to Online or while you have an active job.

We collect location data to:

  • Display your position on the dispatch Live Map for operations staff
  • Assign nearby jobs and provide turn-by-turn routing to job sites
  • Update job progress (for example, en route, arrived, started) when required by the workflow
  • Verify proximity to a job location when starting certain jobs

Location is not collected continuously when you are Offline and have no active job. You can stop background location collection at any time by going Offline in the app or by revoking location permission in your device Settings.

During an active job, your live location may be visible to Clicks operations staff and to the customer assigned to that job. We do not sell your location data to third parties.

How we use personal information

  • Authenticate you and manage your technician account
  • Dispatch, assign, and support roadside assistance jobs
  • Send operational notifications (for example, new job assignments and urgent alerts)
  • Process earnings, activity records, and customer support requests
  • Improve reliability, security, and fraud prevention
  • Comply with legal obligations

How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to operate the service:

  • Clicks operations staff — dispatch, support, and safety monitoring
  • Customers — limited job-related details (such as name, vehicle, and live location during an active job)
  • Service providers — hosting, maps, SMS/OTP delivery, and push notification providers under contract
  • Legal requirements — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We retain account and job records for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, accounting, and operational purposes. Location history associated with jobs is retained according to operational and legal requirements, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Security and data handling

We protect personal and sensitive data using industry-standard safeguards, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit between the app and our servers
  • Access controls limiting employee access to production systems on a need-to-know basis
  • Authentication and authorization for technician and admin accounts
  • Monitoring and logging to detect abuse, outages, and unauthorized access
  • Secure storage of credentials and service keys in protected server environments

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

Your choices and rights

  • Go Offline to stop live location sharing when you are not working
  • Manage notification and location permissions in your device Settings
  • Update profile and vehicle information in the app
  • Request account deletion through the app or by contacting support

Children

Clicks Technician is intended for professional technicians and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date and version number below indicate the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the app or our website.
